KazakhTTS3: Extending the Open-Source KazakhTTS2 Corpus with Synthetic Voices and a Small TTS Baseline

Anonymous submission to Interspeech 2025

📄 Paper

Abstract

We present KazakhTTS3, an extension of the open-source KazakhTTS2 corpus incorporating synthetic voices generated by modern TTS systems. Our corpus includes 12 diverse speakers, expanding the available Kazakh speech data for text-to-speech research. We additionally propose a small TTS baseline model trained on this extended corpus, demonstrating that competitive speech synthesis quality can be achieved even with compact model architectures. We evaluate our system through Mean Opinion Score (MOS) listening tests and provide audio samples below.
